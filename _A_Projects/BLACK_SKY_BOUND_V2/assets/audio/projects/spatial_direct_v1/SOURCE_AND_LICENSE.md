# Spatial direct asset re-exports

These files are deterministic re-exports of retained, attributed source stems already used by Black Sky Bound. Point-source direct layers are mono; selected stereo reflections are preserved separately as environment returns. No new external recording or licence was introduced.

- Mama and fire stems derive from the Pixabay recordings retained under `assets/audio/sources/mama_sampled_v1/originals`.
- Thunder, werewolf, husk, and raider stems derive from the Pixabay recordings documented in `assets/audio/sources/opening_exterior_v1/SOURCE_AND_LICENSE.md`.
- Full source-to-output mappings are generated in `PRODUCTION_ANALYSIS.json`.
- `spatial_direct_v1.lof` opens the master exports together in Audacity for review.
