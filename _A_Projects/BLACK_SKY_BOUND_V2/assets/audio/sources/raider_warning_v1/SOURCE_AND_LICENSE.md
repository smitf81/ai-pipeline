## Authored use in Black Sky Bound

The five production warnings contain no generated voice or synthetic replacement layer. Every audible layer is derived from the preserved recordings:

- `01_real_warning_vocal` - selected warning articulation, lightly darkened and shortened for the windup window;
- `02_real_chest_double` - source-derived lower chest support;
- `03_real_fight_breath` - a contrasting real expelled-breath/grunt layer;
- `04_source_derived_early_room` - short filtered delays made from the main recording rather than generated ambience.

The selected `candidate_b_mud_raider` palette keeps human articulation while adding body and grime. The clean palette was rejected as too much like an isolated recording session; the brute palette was rejected because the raider began to read as a monster.

`audacity_session/raider_warning_v1.lof` is a portable Audacity list-of-files session. Opening it imports the four aligned stem reels and selected master reel into one editable Audacity window.
