## Authored use in Black Sky Bound

The production asset uses no synthetic replacement layer. Every audible layer is derived from the real recording:

- `00_selected_real_heartbeat_window.wav` — the clean eight-beat source selection with a 140 ms wrap crossfade;
- `01_real_heart_close.wav` — valve/tissue articulation, band-limited to 24–950 Hz;
- `02_chest_body.wav` — low chest/body pressure, band-limited to 26–125 Hz;
- `03_tissue_detail.wav` — restrained organic detail for audibility beneath rain, band-limited to 82–720 Hz.

The selected mix is `candidate_b_body_pressure`. It retains the recognisable biological double movement while adding enough body mass for the player-character perspective.
